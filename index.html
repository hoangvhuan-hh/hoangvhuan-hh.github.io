How these are built

Each tool does one job for one kind of person, and says plainly what it does not do. None of them run a server that holds your data: where a tool needs access to something you own, it talks to that service directly from your own account, and the credentials stay with you.

Every tool has a free tier that does real work — not a countdown, not a crippled demo — because the only honest way to find out whether something is worth paying for is to use it on your own data first.
